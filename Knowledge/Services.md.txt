## Business Problem

Many overdue loan accounts cannot be resolved through telecalling alone. Borrowers may be unavailable, have changed their contact details, or require direct engagement. Financial institutions need a structured field collection service to improve recovery efficiency while maintaining professionalism and regulatory compliance.

---

## Service Overview

Field Collections Services involve physical borrower visits by trained recovery executives to facilitate communication, verify borrower status, negotiate repayment solutions, collect payments where authorized, and update lenders with accurate field intelligence. ### What is Portfolio Recovery?

Portfolio Recovery is the structured management of multiple overdue loan accounts to maximize recoveries through prioritization, segmentation, and coordinated execution.

### How is it different from Debt Recovery?

Debt Recovery focuses on individual borrower accounts, while Portfolio Recovery manages an entire collection of delinquent accounts as a strategic business asset.

### Can portfolios be segmented?

Yes. Portfolios are categorized based on ageing, risk level, loan type, outstanding value, geography, and recovery priority.

### Is performance monitored?

Yes. Recovery progress is continuously measured using dashboards, MIS reports, and executive scorecards.

## Business Problem

Financial institutions manage large numbers of overdue accounts that require timely customer engagement. Manual follow-ups often lead to inconsistent communication, low contact rates, poor payment commitments, and delayed collections. A structured call centre operation improves customer engagement and supports early-stage recovery.

---

## Service Overview

Call Centre Collections is a structured tele-collection service that engages borrowers through outbound and inbound communication. The objective is to remind customers about outstanding dues, understand repayment challenges, negotiate payment commitments, and encourage timely repayment while maintaining professionalism and regulatory compliance.

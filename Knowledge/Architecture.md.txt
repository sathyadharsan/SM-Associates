## User Journey Principles

Every page must answer:

- Who is visiting?
- Why are they here?
- What information do they need?
- What builds trust?
- What action should they take?

Support journeys for:

- CEO
- COO
- CFO
- Recovery Head
- Legal Head
- Compliance Officer
- Operations Manager
- HR Candidate
- Partner
- Job Applicant

# Competitive Content Intelligence

Content should be written based on verified market intelligence rather than assumptions.

Competitor research is used to:

- Understand industry terminology
- Discover standard service naming conventions
- Identify market expectations
- Benchmark service positioning
- Improve enterprise messaging
- Identify content gaps
- Strengthen differentiation

Competitor content must never be copied directly.

# Content Positioning Rules

Every service page should answer:

- What problem does this service solve?
- Who needs this service?
- What business value does it provide?
- Where does it fit in the recovery lifecycle?
- Which industries benefit?
- What related services support it?

This creates consistent, buyer-focused service content.

---

# Competitive Benchmarking Rules

When reviewing competitors:

- Study service structure.
- Study terminology.
- Study positioning.
- Study trust signals.
- Study process depth.
- Study technology capabilities.

Do not copy competitor copywriting.

Create original content based on verified business capabilities.

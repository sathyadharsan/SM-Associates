# Phase 5 - Enterprise Content Audit

## Audit Purpose

This document validates the knowledge created during Phases 1–4B.

It does not introduce new business content.

Its purpose is to:

- Validate existing knowledge
- Detect duplicate information
- Identify missing data
- Prioritize business gaps
- Measure website readiness
- Measure SEO readiness
- Measure AI readiness
- Define governance rules

---

## Audit Categories

- Business Readiness
- Website Readiness
- SEO Readiness
- Brand Readiness
- AI Readiness
- Sales Readiness

---

## Validation Rules

Every content asset is classified as:

- Approved
- Needs Improvement
- Client Validation Required
- Missing
- Gap

---

## Audit Deliverables

Maintain audit records for:

- Content Inventory
- Duplicate Detection
- Trust Audit
- Brand Consistency
- Website Readiness
- SEO Readiness
- AI Readiness
- Content Quality
- Governance
- Gap Analysis

---

## Content Governance Rules

Every published content must:

- Be factually verified.
- Be management approved.
- Be legally compliant.
- Follow enterprise terminology.
- Follow brand standards.
- Avoid unsupported claims.

---

## Gap Management

Track:

- Critical Gaps
- High Priority Gaps
- Medium Priority Gaps
- Low Priority Gaps

Every gap must include:

- Owner
- Priority
- Status
- Resolution Date

---

## Content Integrity Policy

Never:

- Invent facts
- Invent statistics
- Invent testimonials
- Invent certifications
- Invent client names
- Invent case studies

Unknown information must always be marked as GAP.

Tone of Voice	The live "Speciality" page uses a dignified, principled tone ("each debtor has the ability to pay if motivated in correct manner") that matches V3's recommended voice well — but the live "Why Choose Us" section uses generic e-commerce language ("Best Price Guarantee," "Best Quality Products") that directly contradicts it.

The fix is largely subtractive (removing the generic boilerplate) rather than additive.

# Company Overview

SM Associates Risk Management Pvt. Ltd. is a professional Recovery and Risk Management organization providing end-to-end business solutions for Banks, NBFCs, Housing Finance Companies, Financial Institutions, and other enterprise clients.

The company specializes in operational recovery, legal recovery coordination, verification services, security solutions, asset management support, and enterprise risk management services.

The organization focuses on delivering compliant, transparent, scalable, and operationally efficient recovery services that help clients improve portfolio performance while maintaining regulatory standards.
